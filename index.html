Ars TechnicaNationwide rollout · 1 of 19

FAA takes AI flight-routing advice nationwide

🧠

The FAA is expanding its AI flight-planning system across the United States. Human staff still review recommendations, and controllers can reject them.

See moreSee less

📰 What happened

The FAA says its $875 million SMART system will now cover all 22 US air-traffic centers. Since starting near Washington, DC, it has rerouted 180 flights, the agency says, saving an average of 10 minutes and nearly 80 gallons of fuel per flight. FAA specialists review its advice, and controllers decide whether to accept it.

🎯 Why it matters to you

It’s a major example of AI being used in real infrastructure, not just chat apps. The reported savings are agency claims, and the air-traffic controllers’ union says it wasn’t involved in designing or testing the system.

✅ What you can do

Watch for independent reporting on safety and fuel savings before treating the FAA’s figures as proven.

TechRadarData exposure · 5 of 19

Grok Build exposure left tens of thousands of apps accessible

🧠

Researchers found many Grok Build apps could be opened without authentication. The company removed its apps after researchers reported the issue, TechRadar says.

See moreSee less

📰 What happened

Researchers identified 43,601 Grok Build web addresses through passive DNS data. 🎯 Why it matters to you

The incident is a warning for anyone using AI coding agents to build apps that handle customer or business data. A hard-to-guess link is not a substitute for access controls.

✅ What you can do

Check that any AI-generated app you publish requires authentication and does not expose private data by default.
