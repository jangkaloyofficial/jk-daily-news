💸 Peso & Rate

Peso closed at P63.25/USD, BSP raised rates 50 bps amid inflation pressure.

Basahin paItago

📰 Ano ang nangyari

On Nov 11 the peso ended at P63.25 per US dollar, 34 centavos weaker than the day before and still above last week’s record low of P64.17. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas raised its benchmark interest rate by 50 basis points to curb inflation. Strong US inflation data boosted demand for the greenback. Economists say the hike may not stop supply‑side price rises like oil.

🎯 Bakit mahalaga sa iyo

A weaker peso raises the cost of imported PC parts and makes loans for building rigs pricier.

✅ Puwede mong gawin

Watch the USD‑PHP rate before ordering components or taking a loan.

📖 Ano ’yong “basis points”?

One basis point equals 0.01% of an interest rate.

⛽

DEPDev says 5‑6% growth possible next year if price pressures drop and El Niño is managed.

Basahin paItago

📰 Ano ang nangyari

The Department of Economy, Planning and Development said the Philippines could grow 5‑6% in 2027 if inflation eases, fuel prices stay low and El Niño risks are contained. Fuel prices have dropped sharply, helping inflation. Q3 growth was 2.1%, below the 4% same period last year, and the economy needs about 6.3% growth in Q4 to meet the lower end of the 2027 target.

🎯 Bakit mahalaga sa iyo

Lower inflation and cheaper fuel can keep the cost of imported tech and electricity down for PC builders.

✅ Puwede mong gawin

Keep an eye on fuel price trends before planning big purchases.

📖 Ano ’yong “El Niño”?

A climate pattern that can raise temperatures and affect agriculture and energy supply.

Retail dollar bonds open for small investors

📈

Gov’t aims to raise at least $200 M via on‑shore retail dollar bonds, min investment $200.

Basahin paItago

📰 Ano ang nangyari

The Treasury will issue Retail Dollar Bond 3 (RDB 3) to raise at least $200 million (about ₱12.8 billion). The auction is set for Nov 19, with issuance on Nov 27. Minimum purchase is $200 (≈₱12,800) and can be bought through BTr’s online portal, GCash GBonds, PDAX, and other apps. The bonds give Filipinos dollar‑denominated returns and diversify savings.

🎯 Bakit mahalaga sa iyo

Earning in dollars can hedge against a weak peso, helping you afford imported PC parts later.

✅ Puwede mong gawin

If interested, compare rates and consider buying via the PDAX app.

📖 Ano ’yong “retail dollar bond”?

A government bond issued in US dollars that regular investors can buy in small amounts.

RCBC lets Filipino freelancers get paid in stablecoins 24/7, auto‑converting to dollar balances.

Basahin paItago

📰 Ano ang nangyari

RCBC announced on Thursday, November 12, 2026 that it now accepts stablecoin transfers from US clients. The bank will automatically convert the stablecoins into dollar balances for Filipino freelancers. This service works 24/7, so freelancers don’t need to manage crypto themselves. It aims to simplify cross‑border payments for those working on platforms like Upwork or Fiverr.

🎯 Bakit mahalaga sa iyo

It gives freelancers a faster, crypto‑free way to receive US payments without dealing with exchanges.

✅ Puwede mong gawin

Check RCBC’s stablecoin payment option and link your freelancer accounts to start receiving payments.

📖 Ano ’yong “stablecoin”?

A cryptocurrency pegged to a stable asset, usually a fiat currency, to reduce price volatility.
